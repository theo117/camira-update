About Camira

Accounting support that goes beyond basic compliance.

Camira Registered Accountants and Auditors is a well-established audit and accounting firm based in Pretoria.

We provide a comprehensive range of services to our clients. Our energetic and positive approach to daily business solutions gives you the upper edge on your competitors.

During the year 2000, Marica Gerber CA (SA), decided to offer her skills to small and medium businesses. She believes these companies deserve the same service levels and quality of work expected from larger audit firms, without the same cost burden.

A team of professionals provides service and advice across accounting, auditing, tax, statutory matters and business support. Continuous training helps the team maintain high technical standards while adding value to your business.

We strive to offer a one stop solution on business related activities. By raising the bar in the financial industry, Camira goes beyond basic services, we create friendships.

Led by experience

Marica Gerber is a registered Accountant and Auditor, a Master Tax Practitioner, and has additional qualifications in Forensic Accounting and B-BBEE.
